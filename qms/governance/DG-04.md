# DG-04: AI records and ISO/IEC 42001 evidence export

**Status:** Controlled-document draft for Quality review

## Required records

Retain the approved AI purpose and risk treatment, tool and version register, validation and release records, configuration history, model-provider state, run and replay hashes, referenced source records, human review, acceptance or rejection, resulting QMS action, monitoring and withdrawal history. Keep source measurements and evidence in their governing Flight records.

## Integrity and access

Every AI action-log event includes its predecessor hash and a SHA-256 hash over the canonical event. Verification checks sequence, actor, event hashes, skill run input and output hashes, and referenced records. Access follows the workspace's role and account controls. A broken chain fails verification and is reported for investigation.

## Export

An authorized Quality role produces an ISO/IEC 42001:2023 evidence JSON from the current validated workspace. It includes the build identity, export identity and time, organization context, current risk register, tool versions, controls, configuration state, training and competency evidence, run and decision metrics, action log, and verification result. The export includes hashes and a summary; it does not replace source records or their approval chains.

## Limitations

An export is a point-in-time evidence extract. It does not certify conformity, establish that a management system is effective, or replace an independent review.
