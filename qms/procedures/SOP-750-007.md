# SOP-750-007: Control of AI-generated tools

**Status:** Draft for Skyryse Quality review

This draft is the Skyryse-numbered companion for AI-generated and AI-assisted tools. It must be reviewed against the released Flight quality procedures before use. DG-01 through DG-04 retain their own document identifiers where no existing Skyryse procedure is equivalent.

## Required controls

1. Identify the tool, version, owner, purpose, input boundary, output boundary, permitted users and prohibited decisions.
2. Validate the tool and its refusal paths before release. Revalidate changes to the tool, model, provider, prompt, schema, trigger or target workflow.
3. Keep automation output as a draft. A qualified person independently checks the source evidence and uses the governing Flight approval path.
4. Do not let a tool release a WI, sign an FAA or quality record, approve its own analysis, inspect its own work, close its own finding, or bypass own-work or separation-of-duties controls.
5. Use a deterministic method by default. Model adapters are off until specifically approved and configured through the server secret setting.
6. Retain version, run time, actor, input hash, output hash, source references, human disposition, and a chained action-log hash. Do not place secrets or unnecessary raw customer data in the log.
7. Treat `[confirm]` markers as incomplete prompts. They cannot be accepted as evidence or findings.
8. Disable a tool and preserve its records if its integrity, validation, logging or provider behavior cannot be verified.

The DG governance series and DG-01 job description template are controlled through the Flight document register. This SOP remains a draft until separately authored, reviewed and released by three different people.
